# Focal length of a convex lens

An interactive lesson on finding the focal length of a convex lens by the u–v method on an optical bench, and the power of the lens.

Live: https://sonicunligmated.github.io/convex-lens-lesson/

## Contents

- **Practical**: a distance-object scene for a rough focal length, then an optical bench with a lamp house, a convex lens and a screen. The screen shows the real image the lens forms (inverted, scaled by m = v/u and blurred by the focus error). Readings fill an observation table, a 1/v against 1/u graph gives the intercepts, and a calculation leads to the result and the power in dioptres. A guided tutorial walks through the whole procedure.
- **Aim and outcomes**, **Theory** (ray diagrams, the lens formula, u + v ≥ 4F, power, sources of error), **Method**, **Observation and calculation** (a worked example with example values) and **MCQs**.

## Running locally

It is a static site with no build step:

```
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000/`.
